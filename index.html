Voice Agent Eval

An evaluation platform for a healthcare voice agent, built in a strict 8 hour timebox. A transcript can sound perfect while nothing changed, so ground truth is the simulated world state, and the headline metric is false completion: the agent said it was done and the world says otherwise.

Comparing two agent versions, headline metrics moved 1 to 3 points while recall on calls that must escalate fell 87.

Why sliced reporting. An aggregate hid the safety regression completely, so regressions are listed before averages by design. Evaluator agreement with human labels went from a Cohen's kappa of −0.05 to 0.86, and a deterministic quote check caught the LLM judge inventing a citation it labeled verbatim.

FastAPI, SQLite, Next.js, LLM as judge, 45 tests, 17 labeled scenarios

ActionGuard

A deterministic guardrail for a computer use agent operating a claims system. Fourteen rules sort every action into allow, escalate, abstain or block, stopping payments to excluded providers, leaks of patient data and duplicate replays.

Against 26 indirect prompt injection attacks the detector caught 76.9%, yet 100% were contained by verb level authority controls.

Why authority over detection. Detection missed every adaptive semantic attack, so safety rests on what each verb is allowed to do, not on spotting the attack. Standard library only, 83 tests, tamper evident audit trace.

Fit Score

A physician job match card that shows its own uncertainty. When a criterion is unknown, guessing it misleads and scoring it as zero punishes small and rural sites for missing paperwork, so unknowns are excluded and shown as coverage and a floor to ceiling band.
