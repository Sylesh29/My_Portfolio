Voice Agent Eval

An evaluation platform for a healthcare voice agent, built in a strict 8 hour timebox. A transcript can sound perfect while nothing changed, so ground truth is the simulated world state, and the headline metric is false completion: the agent said it was done and the world says otherwise.

Comparing two agent versions, the headline metric moved about 2 points while recall on calls that must escalate fell 87.

Why sliced reporting. An aggregate hid the safety regression completely, so regressions are listed before averages by design. A deterministic quote check also caught the LLM judge inventing a citation it claimed was verbatim.

FastAPI, SQLite, Next.js, LLM as judge, 45 tests, 17 labeled scenarios

Aug 2026

Fit Score

A physician job match card that shows its own uncertainty. When a criterion is unknown, guessing it misleads and scoring it as zero punishes small and rural sites for missing paperwork, so unknowns are excluded and shown as coverage and a floor to ceiling band.

A site scoring 94 with 43% coverage can be a worse job than one scoring 67 with full coverage, and the band shows it before you read a number.

Process. Synthetic data only, with 43 written design decisions in the repo.

AuditGuard AI

Data integrity triage for manufacturing records ahead of an inspection. Upload a production CSV and a four stage pipeline flags problems with explainable rules, ranks them by audit risk, proposes corrections without touching the original, and produces a report a compliance officer can sign.
